Preparing for your appointment

  1. Gather your records. Bring earlier reports, X-rays, scans and a list of medicines.
  2. Note your symptoms. When did it start, what makes it better or worse, and what can you no longer do?
  3. Wear easy clothing. Loose clothes make it easier for the doctor to examine the joint.
  4. Write your questions. A short list keeps the visit focused on what matters to you.
  5. Arrive a little early. Allow time for forms and registration.

General orthopaedic care

Everyday habits that support bones and joints.

Joint care

  • Keep moving in ways that do not hurt
  • Warm up before exercise
  • Keep a healthy body weight to reduce load on joints

Bone health

  • Eat enough calcium and vitamin D foods
  • Do regular weight-bearing activity
  • Avoid smoking and heavy drinking

Posture and movement

  • Break up long periods of sitting
  • Lift with your legs, not your back
  • Set up your desk so screens are at eye level

Recovery and rehabilitation

General guidance. Your own plan from your doctor always comes first.

Follow your plan

Take medicines and do exercises as your care team advised, and attend every review.

Build up gradually

Increase activity in small steps. Do not push through sharp pain.

Rest and sleep

Healing needs rest. Ask your doctor how to position and support the limb.

Know when to call

Contact the clinic if pain, swelling, redness or fever increases, or numbness appears.
